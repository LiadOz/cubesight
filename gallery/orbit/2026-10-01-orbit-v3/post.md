Two components (the cube and the orbit), five pieces (header, key bar, coach line, actions, chip), every flow mapped to the ring, and one storyboard from solve to results to history to a drill and back.

Three directions: **A one orbit**, **B orbit + rail**, **C orbit as navigation**.

**Decision.** A chosen: the cube is the centrepiece. The user loved **A-05** (the connector between the coach line and the marker on the ring) and **A-10** (a past solve). B and C stay as reference.
