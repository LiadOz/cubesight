Pick by looking at **TC-00** (Q1 to Q3). One readout in three sizes (XL under the cube, L in drills, M inline) and the A-05 coach line with its dotted connector, built from approved widgets only.

3 options:

1. **quiet**: plain digits, a sub line only when it helps; the sentence in a rail (top or bottom slot, mirrored on the right), a dotted curve to its marker. This is A-05 as drawn.
2. **tone**: amber/red digits with a companion (+2, DNF); the sentence sits at the marker's own height with a straight dotted line.
3. **two-line slot**: a permanent consequence line under the time; the sentence is attached to the marker's label, no line.

Per option, full size (IDs `TC-<option><n>`): 1 solving, 2 inspection and overtime (+1 +2, DNF), 3 every state in XL/L/M, 4 a drill (L and M), 5 phone, 6 results with the coach, 7 praise and fix and the connector fading in and out, 8 a crowded ring (14 markers), 9 phone coach. Light sheet: TC-91.

I recommend option 1 (it is the form you loved in A-05; Q2 and Q3 can be mixed in freely).
