One look for list rows, the mini Orbit glyph, stats, tags and section heads, shown on history and a drill case list.

3 options:

1. open rows: no frame, only the selected row has a fill; stats are bare numbers
2. banded rows: every row a soft block; tags are pills; stats are tiles
3. hairline list: rows cut by hairlines; stats read as one line

Pick in image L-00-decide.
