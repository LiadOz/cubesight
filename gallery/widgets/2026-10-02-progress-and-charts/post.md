Pick by looking at **PC-00** (Q1 to Q3). Every bar, panel and one-off chart is replaced by an Orbit form (PC-01 lists each one); the options only differ in what happens to the long-term trend.

3 options:

1. **Orbit only**: mini Orbits for the recent rounds and one per session; no line anywhere.
2. **Orbit + tick dial**: the trend is a dial of ticks on an arc (longer = slower); still no line or axes.
3. **Orbit + one minimal line**: one hairline of the ao12, the only chart on the site.

Per option, full size (IDs `PC-<option><n>`): 1 end of a drill round, 2 progress page, 3 phone. Shared: PC-01 what replaces what, PC-02 a round in progress (A-08), PC-91 light sheet.

**W-37 wipe handle** (A ink knob, B hairline + bottom grabber, C the Orbit caret): PC-41 (five states), PC-42 to 44 (on the compare page), PC-45 (phone touch target).

I recommend option 1 and handle C. Option 3 is the fallback only if you want a real long-term curve.
