One system for sections, panels, drawers and dialogs, shown on the settings drawer and the delete dialog.

3 options:

1. frameless: sections are space + a head; only lists and logs get a panel
2. soft panels: groups and disclosures sit in a soft fill with a hairline
3. hairline rules: no fills; structure by hairlines

Pick in image C-00-decide.
