The user's decision after living with both themes: **"the orbit around the cube is the centre of everything."**

- Orbit is now the direction for the whole site: solve, results, history, drills.
- Orbit dark is designed first; light follows.
- Mono stays as a working theme but is **superseded**: it no longer drives decisions.

This led to orbit-v3, a design round that starts from the cube and the orbit as the only two components.
