Three complete visual directions for the Brain (the solve screen), each with the same eleven frames: idle, scramble, inspection, overtime, variants, solving, skip, results, mobile, settings, motion.

- **A: Mono.** Monkeytype-like: minimal, one accent, big tabular numbers.
- **B: Pit wall.** F1 timing screen: dense, data-forward.
- **C: Orbit.** A ring around the cube is the clock and the stage timeline.

Shown here: the results frame of each (A-08, B-08, C-08). Every direction has its own post on its own branch.

**Decision.** B was rejected. The user loved C (Orbit) but could not choose between A and C, so both were kept as two switchable themes, each in dark and light, dark first.
