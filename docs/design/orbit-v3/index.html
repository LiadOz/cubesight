Design system and flow

Two components (cube, orbit), five pieces (header, key bar, coach line, actions, chip), every flow mapped to the ring, and one storyboard from solve to results to history to a drill and back.

One giant cube in one open dial; the gap at the bottom is the one slot for the number that matters. History: timeline by session with mini orbits.

B · Orbit + rail

A full ring plus one thin borderless rail for the words. History: calendar where every day is a ring.

C · Orbit as navigation

Inner ring = stages (tap to open); outer ring = sections, actions, answers, transport. History: grid of orbit cards.
